## Type contract — fixed before fan-out, do not redesign

`evals/types.ts` on the base branch already defines `SourcedFact<T>`,
`FundingFact`, `EmployeeBand`, `TickerFact`, `ExpectedFacts` and `GroundTruth`.
t02 codes its scorer against these; t04 fills data into them. Neither invents a
shape. The load-bearing rule:

> Every field of `ExpectedFacts` is optional, and **an absent field is not
> graded.** A fact that cannot be pinned to a source URL is omitted, never
> guessed — otherwise a soft label silently becomes a wrong grade.

### What generalises to the next fan-out

- **Three of four briefing claims the orchestrator wrote were wrong** —
  `tech-stack` vs the real `tech_stack`, Lovable's funding date, Cal.com's
  maturity — and the agents were right each time. Verify a brief's factual
  claims against the repo and primary sources before treating an agent's
  deviation as a mistake.
- **A delegate asserting it verified something is not verification.** Two
  separate tasks reported audits they had not fully performed. Where the claim
  matters, build the mechanical check instead of asking again.
- **`idle` is not completion, and neither is a written result file.** t01 went
  idle without updating its result after a follow-up, having actually done the
  work; the commit was the truth. Read the diff.
- **Cost regressions hide inside plausible refactors.** The logo change read as
  a clean improvement in its own result file and in its diff. It only looks
  wrong once you ask what it costs per call.
